AI Cricket Bowling
Analysis System

Uses AI pose detection to track the bowler's arm, detect the release point, then follow the ball.

🤖 How it works

1
AI detects the bowler
MoveNet tracks 17 body keypoints in real-time
2
Tracks the bowling arm
Follows shoulder→elbow→wrist through the delivery
3
Detects ball release
When wrist velocity peaks and arm passes vertical
4
Tracks the ball
Color segmentation follows ball down the pitch

📐 Setup tips

Position your phone side-on to the pitch, ~15-20m away. Use a tripod or prop it up — keep it still. Good daylight works best.
